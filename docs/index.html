[ Xiaomi Bloat Eliminator ]

Made by gpsn0w & Claude

Keep Privacy First Дръжте поверителността първа.

Remove pre-installed bloatware from Xiaomi & Redmi devices running MIUI or HyperOS.
No root required. Works on Mac, Linux and Windows.

Tested on Xiaomi 14 — HyperOS 3.0 ✓

Маха предварително инсталирания bloatware от Xiaomi & Redmi устройства с MIUI или HyperOS.
Не е нужен root. Работи на Mac, Linux и Windows.

Тествано на Xiaomi 14 — HyperOS 3.0 ✓

Mac / Linux
bash <(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gpsn0w/MiNator/main/install.sh)
Windows (PowerShell)
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gpsn0w/MiNator/main/install.ps1 | iex
