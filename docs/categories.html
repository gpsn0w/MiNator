Categories

MiNator asks you about each category separately — you choose what to remove. Here's exactly what each one contains and why.

1 — Ads & Analytics Privacy Risk

Spyware baked into the system. Tracks your usage and sends it to Xiaomi servers.

PackageWhat it does
com.miui.analyticsTracks everything you do and sends it to Xiaomi
com.miui.msaMi Service Framework — injects ads directly into the system
com.miui.systemAdSolutionLiterally has "ads" in the name

2 — Facebook & LinkedIn Privacy Risk

Pre-installed social bloatware that runs in the background without your consent.

PackageWhat it does
com.facebook.systemSpies on you even without Facebook installed
com.facebook.appmanagerCan install Facebook apps without asking you
com.facebook.servicesCollects your data even without an account
com.linkedin.androidPre-installed, nobody asked for it

3 — Mi Game Center Bloatware

Useless if you don't play Xiaomi games. Takes up space and runs background services.

PackageWhat it does
com.xiaomi.migameserviceMi Game Service — Xiaomi's game store nobody uses
com.xiaomi.glgmMi Game Center — partner in crime of the above

4 — Mi Browser PRIVACY NIGHTMARE

Every site you visit gets sent to Xiaomi. Yes, all of them. Yes, that one too.

PackageWhat it does
com.mi.globalbrowserPRIVACY NIGHTMARE — sends your entire browsing history to Xiaomi
com.mi.globalbrowser.miniSame story, just smaller. Still spying on you.

5 — Mi Music & Video Bloatware

Ad-infested media players. Use Spotify and VLC instead.

PackageWhat it does
com.miui.playerMi Music — music player with built-in ads
com.miui.videoMi Video — video player with built-in ads

6 — GetApps / Mi Store Bloatware

Xiaomi's own app store — full of spam notifications and push ads.

PackageAlt packageWhat it does
com.xiaomi.mipickscom.miui.mipicksGetApps — Xiaomi Play Store, spam notifications

7 — Safe System Bloat Safe to remove

Bug reports and user tracking sent to Xiaomi. Safe to remove — does not affect core phone functions.

PackageWhat it does
com.miui.bugreportAuto-sends "bug reports" to Xiaomi without asking you
com.miui.feedbackSends how you use your phone as "feedback"
com.miui.usertrackLiterally called "usertrack" — need we say more?

8 — Mi Cloud Backup Optional — double confirmation

⚠ Warning

Mi Cloud is connected to Find My Device. Removing com.miui.cloudbackup may affect your ability to locate your phone remotely. MiNator asks twice before removing this one.

ℹ Note

com.miui.cloudservice is intentionally NOT removed — it is required for Find My Device to work.

PackageWhat it does
com.miui.cloudbackupBacks up your data to Chinese servers
